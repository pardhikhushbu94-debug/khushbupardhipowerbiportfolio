# Khushbu Pardhi - Senior Power BI Developer Portfolio 🚀

> **Live Portfolio:** [https://pardhikhushbu94-debug.github.io/](https://pardhikhushbu94-debug.github.io/)

A modern, high-performance, and responsive portfolio webpage for **Khushbu Pardhi**, a Senior Power BI Developer & Semantic Modeling Specialist with 4+ years of dedicated BI engineering experience and 5+ years of prior US Mortgage financial risk underwriting experience.

## 🌟 Portfolio Highlights

- **Interactive Dark & Light Theme**: Seamless mode switcher with persisted preference (`localStorage`) and system preference fallback.
- **Her Unique Journey**: Highlights her progression from 5+ years of meticulous financial underwriting (conventional US mortgages, DTI/LTV ratios, credit & asset audits) to enterprise Power BI semantic modeling, star schemas, and automated reporting.
- **Live Power BI KPI Explorer**: In-browser interactive dashboard simulator allowing recruiters and engineering managers to toggle between:
  - *Retail Sales & Inventory Intelligence*
  - *Financial Performance & Budget Variance Analytics*
  - *Workforce & Attrition Intelligence System*
  - Live metric updates, category bar breakdowns, and authentic DAX calculation snippets.
- **Flagship Enterprise Case Studies**: Deep technical breakdowns of her work at Dhandhania Infotech across Retail, Corporate Finance, and Human Capital.
- **Technical Skills Matrix**: Comprehensive categorization of BI tools, DAX time intelligence, star/snowflake modeling, Power Query M, SQL Server, and Power BI Service administration (RLS, Gateways).
- **Direct Contact & Copy Utilities**: One-click email copy, telephone links, interactive message dispatch, and social profiles.

## 🚀 Running Locally

Simply open `index.html` in any web browser, or launch a local static server:

```bash
# Using Python
python -m http.server 8000

# Using Node.js npx
npx serve .
```

Visit `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
